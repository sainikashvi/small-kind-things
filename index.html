How the chain works

You get a card. The card is your turn.

  1. Do one small kind thingShare notes, say thank you, listen for five minutes. Small is the point.
  2. Hand over the cardGive the card to the person you helped, so they can pass it on too.
  3. Scan and add your linkThe QR on the card opens a short form. No name needed.

Keep it safe

Kindness works best when everyone feels comfortable.

  • Keep help small: notes, time, food, a listening ear. No money.
  • Meet new people only in public places, never at their home.
  • Ask before sharing anyone’s photo or story.
  • We never ask for passwords, OTPs or bank details. If anyone does in our name, ignore it.
  • If someone is in danger or distress, don’t handle it alone. Tele-MANAS (14416) is free, 24x7.
  • Help because you want to, not for the camera.
